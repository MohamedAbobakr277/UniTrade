🎨 Palette: Added tooltip and ARIA label to ItemCard favorite button

**💡 What:**
Added a `Tooltip` component and an `aria-label` attribute to the favorite (`IconButton`) on the `ItemCard` component. The tooltip and label dynamically switch between "Add to favorites" and "Remove from favorites" based on the `isFavorite` state.

**🎯 Why:**
The favorite button relied solely on a heart icon (filled or outlined) without any descriptive text. This made its purpose ambiguous for some users and completely inaccessible to screen readers. Adding a tooltip provides an explicit visual hint, and the ARIA label ensures accessibility.
